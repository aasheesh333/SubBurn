Privacy Policy

SubBurn is an AI & SaaS subscription tracker built local-first.

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The short version: your subscriptions never leave your phone. There is no account, no cloud database, and no way for us to read your ledger. We do not sell data and the app has no ads.

1. Who we are

SubBurn is published by an independent developer. For any privacy question, contact aasheeshkatheriya@gmail.com.

2. What the app stores on your device

All subscription data is written to a local database (Hive) inside the app's private storage:

  • tool name, vendor, price, currency and billing cycle
  • start date and next renewal date
  • your usage-guilt choice (daily driver / barely touch it)
  • reminder on/off per subscription
  • your display currency and a random install identifier

This data is not transmitted anywhere. It is excluded from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

3. Network requests the app makes

DestinationWhat is sentWhy
Cloudflare R2 (static CDN) A plain HTTPS GET. No identifiers, no cookies, no subscription data. Download the public preset catalogue and a USD→INR exchange rate, cached for 7 days.
Firebase Analytics Two product events — sub_added and roast_card_shared — with coarse buckets (category, cycle, price range, burn range) and an app-instance id. Understand whether the core flow works.
Firebase Crashlytics Crash stack traces, app version, device model, OS version, and a crash-installation id. Fix crashes. Never includes subscription names or amounts.
Firebase Remote Config App version, platform, and a random app-instance id. Adjust two configuration values without shipping an update.
Firebase Cloud Messaging / OneSignal A push token and the random install id. Subscription data is never included. Deliver broadcast product announcements, if you allow notifications.

4. What we never collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • Your subscription list, prices, vendors, or the amount you spend.
  • Your location, photos, files, SMS, call logs or clipboard.
  • Advertising identifiers. The app contains no ad SDK and no tracking SDK beyond the Firebase services listed above.

5. Permissions and why they are requested

  • Notifications (optional) — renewal reminders 48 h and 24 h before a charge. Scheduled with inexact alarms; the app does not request the “alarms & reminders” special access.
  • Internet — the CDN and Firebase requests described above.

Declining notifications leaves the rest of the app fully functional.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with advertisers or data brokers. The only processors are Google (Firebase) and Cloudflare, acting as infrastructure providers under their own terms.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Local ledger: deleted when you delete a subscription, use Controls → Delete all subscriptions, or uninstall the app.
  • Analytics and crash data: retained by Firebase for 14 months (Analytics) and 90 days (Crashlytics), then deleted automatically.
  • Push identity: a random install id, removed when you uninstall or opt out of notifications.

To request deletion of any server-side analytics associated with your app instance, email us and we will action it within 30 days.

8. Children

SubBurn is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Security

All network traffic uses HTTPS and the app blocks cleartext traffic. Local data sits inside the app sandbox, which other apps cannot read on a non-rooted device.

10. Changes

If this policy changes materially, the “Last updated” date above changes and the new version is published at this URL before the relevant app update ships.
